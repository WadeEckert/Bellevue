## Kirkpatrick Signature Series

Completed during the **Fall 2026 Term**, the Kirkpatrick Signature Series examined the values, institutions, and responsibilities that shape a good society. Through discussion posts, research-based papers, and reflection assignments, I considered how individual choices connect with community needs and public life.

Topics included:

- Individual rights and civic responsibilities
- Individualism and communitarianism
- Fairness, justice, equality, and opportunity
- National identity, pluralism, assimilation, and globalization
- Social movements, civic participation, and trust in institutions
- Evaluating competing arguments and explaining my own positions using course readings

### Civic Engagement Project

For the course's Civic Engagement Project, I developed a **Python desktop application** to help Membership Services staff at **Fund for the Public Interest Network** prepare membership CSV files for upload to the organization's CRM. I completed this project as a separate volunteer contribution outside my regular job responsibilities.

The application supports column mapping, routine formatting cleanup, data validation, duplicate review, and manual correction of uncertain records before exporting a CSV in the required template layout.

The project connected software development with community service by addressing a repetitive administrative task for staff supporting environmental advocacy. It also gave me experience gathering requirements, incorporating user feedback, testing application behavior, and preparing instructions for an end user.

The project workbook connected this experience with my values of **learning, responsibility, and independence**.
